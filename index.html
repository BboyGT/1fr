Four demos from the CSS-Tricks article by Godstime Aburu. Each one isolates a different part of the bug and its fix.

The Bug — Image Overflow

A 400px image inside a 1fr column sets the track minimum to 400px. The container boundary means nothing to it.

Nested Grid Toggle
